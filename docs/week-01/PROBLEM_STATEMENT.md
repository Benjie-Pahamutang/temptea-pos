# Week 1: Problem Statement & Record Types

## Problem Statement
TEMPTEA POS is an enterprise Point of Sale and inventory tracking application designed for local tea and beverage shops. Small beverage businesses struggle to manage fast-paced daily ordering while maintaining accurate real-time inventory counts across custom drink configurations (sweetness levels, toppings, sizes). TEMPTEA POS provides a streamlined interface for cashiers to process transactions rapidly and for administrators to track stock, sales history, and product performance.

## Managed Record Types (CRUD)
1. **Products & Inventory Items:** Tea bases, toppings, milk/syrup inventory, and pricing structures.
2. **Sales Transactions / Orders:** Cashier sales records, line items, item customizations, and total amounts.
3. **Customer Loyalty / Accounts:** Customer contact details, purchase history, and reward points.
4. **User / Cashier Profiles:** Staff credentials, role permissions (Admin vs. Cashier), and shift logs.
