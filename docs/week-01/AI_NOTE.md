# Week 1: AI Brainstorming Log

## Brainstorming Prompt Used
> "Brainstorm 5 small, real-world local business problems in the Philippines suitable for a CRUD-based desktop application finishable within 12 weeks."

## AI-Generated Candidate Ideas
1. Barbershop appointment and queue scheduling system.
2. **Sari-Sari store / Milk tea shop inventory + sales log (Selected: TEMPTEA POS).**
3. Community library book lending and return tracker.
4. Tutoring center session scheduler and student payment log.
5. Local bakery custom order and ingredient stock tracker.

## Decision Summary
The team selected the Milk Tea / Beverage POS concept because it presents a clear CRUD structure (Products, Orders, Customers) and fits the real-world operational needs of local tea shops.
